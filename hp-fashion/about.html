A dress store,
run like a workshop.

HP Fashion opened on Cross Cut Road in 2009 with one rail of shirts and a borrowed sewing machine. The machine is still upstairs. There are now five more beside it.

Why we started

Because nothing ever fit.

Ready-made clothing in India is cut for an average nobody actually is. You either paid a tailor twice — once at the shop, once to fix it — or you wore something slightly wrong.

So we put the tailors in the building. Fifteen years later it is still the thing about the shop worth explaining.

The rules, in full

What we promise, and what we don't.

Made to order
Describe what you want and we quote in two working days. Half up front, balance on collection.
Exchange
Seven days, unworn, tags on. Bridal and made-to-measure pieces cannot be exchanged, which we say out loud before you order.
Refunds
Store credit rather than cash, except where the garment is faulty. We would rather fix it than take it back.
Pricing
One price on the tag, inclusive of tax. Struck-through figures on this site are the honest earlier price, not a decoration.
Bridal orders
Booked with a deposit, eleven weeks for zardozi work, three fittings included. Deposits are adjustable against anything else in the shop.
Delivery
Same day inside Coimbatore, two to four days elsewhere in Tamil Nadu.
