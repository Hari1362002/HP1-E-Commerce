The alterations room

Six tailors, one floor above the shop.

A hem takes an hour, a jacket takes three days, and a bridal lehenga gets as many fittings as it needs. We do not charge for any of it — not the first time, and not in four years when the trousers need letting out.

It is the reason people who moved to Bangalore still send their shirts back to us.

How the shop works

01

Measured properly

Every piece is fitted on the floor before it is bagged. Nothing goes home on a guess.

02
