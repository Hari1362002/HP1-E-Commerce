Clinical & support services

Everything the hospital actually does

Clinical care, diagnostics, health checks, insurance, room tariffs and the small practical services that a patient's family needs. Prices below are current as of August 2026 and are revised once a year in April.

Round the clock

Services that never close

These six run twenty-four hours a day, every day of the year, including Deepavali and Pongal.

Emergency & trauma

Six triage bays and a resuscitation room with a consultant, anaesthetist and radiographer on site through the night.

Intensive care

Thirty-two ICU beds across medical, surgical, coronary and neonatal units, with a one-to-one nurse ratio in critical beds.

Laboratory

NABL-accredited, with a stat bench for emergency samples that turns around a critical result in under forty minutes.

Pharmacy

In-house pharmacy at the main entrance stocking around 3,200 items, including emergency antidotes and antivenom.

Ambulance

Four vehicles, two of them advanced life support with a ventilator, defibrillator and a trained paramedic on board.

Blood bank access

A standing arrangement with the Coimbatore district blood centre, plus an on-site storage unit for the common groups.

Health checks

Ten packages, fixed prices

No add-ons at the counter. All packages need an eight-hour fast and take about three hours. Book a morning slot — the lab runs its main batch at 10 AM and you will get the report the same evening.

Insurance & payment

Cashless, or a bill you can read

The insurance desk is the first counter on the ground floor, open 8 AM to 8 PM. Bring the policy card and a photo ID and the pre-authorisation goes out the same visit. For emergency admissions the desk works on it while treatment is already under way — nobody waits for paperwork.

  • Empanelled with 34 insurers and third-party administrators
  • Registered under the Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme
  • Central and state government employee schemes accepted
  • Corporate tie-ups for pre-employment and annual staff checks
  • Cash, UPI, credit and debit cards, and NEFT at the billing counter

For a written estimate before a planned admission, ask at the billing counter or email info@velhospital.in with the procedure name.
