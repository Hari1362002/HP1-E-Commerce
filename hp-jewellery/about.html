The bench on Oppanakara

My grandfather rented half a room here in 1998 and made thali chains for other people's shops. Three of the goldsmiths who trained under him are still at the bench today, and two of them now train everyone else.

What we actually do

Not a shop that buys in

Most jewellers on this street are retailers — they order from Rajkot or Thrissur and put a markup on it. We do both: we buy in the plain chain that nobody gains anything by making twice, and we make everything else ourselves, upstairs, where you can watch through the glass if you ask.

That is the whole difference. When a prong lifts on a ring we made, the person who set it is a floor away and it is fixed the same afternoon.

The long version

Twenty-eight years

1998

Half a room, one bench

P. Hariharan starts making thali chains to order for other shops on Oppanakara Street. No shopfront, no sign — the work is passed along by word of mouth from three retailers.

2006

The first counter

A twelve-foot shopfront opens downstairs. The workshop stays upstairs, which turns out to matter: customers start asking to see it, and we start letting them.

2014

Hallmarking, before it was law

We begin BIS hallmarking every single piece, seven years before it became mandatory for most categories. It costs us margin on the light pieces and we do it anyway.

2019

The itemised invoice

Metal, stones, wastage and making charges get split onto separate lines. Several customers ask us to put the old single figure back. We don't.

2023

Wax you can try on

A resin printer arrives, so bespoke commissions start with a piece you can wear around the house for a day before anyone melts gold for it.

Today

Six stores, one workshop

Coimbatore, Chennai, Madurai, Erode, Salem and Tiruppur. Everything sold in all six is still finished on the same floor above the first shop.

Sourcing

We can name the supplier

"Ethically sourced" means nothing on its own, so here is the specific version.

  1. 01

    Diamonds

    Bought through two Surat cutters we have used since 2011, both Kimberley-compliant and both willing to be named on request. Every stone over 0.30ct arrives with an IGI or GIA report.

  2. 02

    Gold

    Refined bullion from an LBMA Good Delivery refiner, plus customer exchange metal — which is the greenest gold there is, because it is already out of the ground.

  3. 03

    Coloured stones

    Zambian emerald and Sri Lankan sapphire, bought directly at origin twice a year. If a stone is treated, the tag says how.

  4. 04

    The people

    Forty-two salaried goldsmiths, not piece-rate contractors. Provident fund, insurance and a five-and-a-half day week — unusual on this street, and the reason nobody leaves.
