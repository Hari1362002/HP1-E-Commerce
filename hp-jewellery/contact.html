Before you ask

The usual questions

Ask us for a free plastic sizer and we'll post it anywhere in India. Wrapping thread around your finger is famously unreliable — it stretches, and it always reads a size small. If you already own a ring that fits, bring it in and we'll measure it on the mandrel in thirty seconds.

The labour of turning bullion into a piece. Ours run 8% on plain chain and up to 22% on hand-set temple work, and they appear as their own line on the invoice next to the metal weight and the stone value. If a jeweller quotes you a single per-gram figure, the making charge is inside it — ask what it is.

Yes. We'll assay it in front of you, pay the day's rate for the assayed purity, and there's no deduction for wastage on hallmarked pieces. Unhallmarked gold has to be melted to establish purity, so that takes a day and the rate depends on what comes out.

  • A bridal set or haram: six to eight weeks
