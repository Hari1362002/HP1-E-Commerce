How joining works

Four steps,
one week

  • Day one — the screen. Thirty minutes of movement testing plus a conversation about your history, your schedule and what you actually want.
  • Day two — your first session. Free, coached, and a real workout, not a tour of the machines.
  • Day three — the plan. Written up, loaded into the app, and explained to you in person before you start.
  • Every month after. A coach reviews your logs, changes the loads and tells you what is next.
