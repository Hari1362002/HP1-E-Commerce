How we hire

Certification is
the minimum

A certificate proves someone passed an exam. It does not prove they can spot a shifting knee from across the room, or tell a 55-year-old with a stiff back what to do next. So every coach here does a six-week paid trial on the floor before they get their own members.

  • Recognised certification. NSCA, ACE, ACSM or ISSA — no weekend courses.
  • Six weeks shadowing. Under a senior coach, on real members, before they programme alone.
  • Continuing education. Two workshops a year, paid for by the gym.
  • They train here too. Every coach on this page is also a member.
