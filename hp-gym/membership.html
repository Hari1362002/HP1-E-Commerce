Questions

Before you join

The six things people ask at the front desk every week.

Is there a joining fee or a lock-in period?

Neither. Memberships run month to month. Tell the desk seven days before your renewal date and it simply stops — no cancellation fee, no retention call.

What actually happens in the free first session?

About ninety minutes: a thirty-minute movement screen and a conversation about your history and schedule, then a real coached workout. You get the screen results either way, even if you never come back.

I have never lifted before. Will I be out of place?

Roughly half our members had never touched a barbell when they joined. The first four weeks are coached start to finish, and the beginners' lift class at 7:30 am exists for precisely this.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes — up to sixty days a year for travel, illness or exams, at no charge. The frozen days get added to the end of your term.

Do you sell supplements?

No. We will tell you honestly what is worth buying (usually whey and creatine, sometimes neither) and where to get it cheaper than we could sell it to you.

Is there parking?

Two-wheeler parking is free and covered. There are twelve car spaces at the back off Trichy Road, first come first served, and street parking after 9 pm.
