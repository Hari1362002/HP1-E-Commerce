Why HP Gym

A coached floor,
not a card swipe

Plenty of gyms sell you a membership and leave you to work it out from YouTube. We do the opposite. Your first four weeks are coached start to finish, and after that a trainer still writes and reviews your programme every month — whether or not you pay for personal training.

  • Movement screen first. Thirty minutes before you touch a barbell, so the plan fits your shoulders, hips and history.
  • Everything is logged. Your loads live in an app, so week four is measurably heavier than week one.
  • Equipment that lasts. Four power racks, two platforms, calibrated plates, dumbbells to 60 kg.
  • Open 5 am to 10:30 pm. Shift workers, students and 9-to-6 folk all get a slot that works.
